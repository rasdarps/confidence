DARPPOS CREDIT - REPORTS VIDEO RECORDING SCRIPT
================================================

PURPOSE
-------
This companion script focuses on the reports related to the workflows in the
DarpPOS Credit walkthrough. Bracketed text is an on-screen direction; quoted
text is suggested narration. Use the report names and filters that are
actually available to the signed-in demo account.


BEFORE RECORDING REPORTS
------------------------
- Use a demo environment with fictional data and a date range containing
  sample sales, purchases, payments, expenses, stock movements, and
  reconciliations.
- Sign in with an account that has access to the reports being shown.
- Clear report filters before beginning each demonstration. Apply one or two
  useful filters on camera, then show the resulting rows and totals.
- Use dates and figures visible on screen. Avoid claiming that all reports
  support the same filters, totals, exports, or print options.
- Reports are permission-controlled. If a report is not available, skip it
  or demonstrate it with an appropriately authorized demo account.
- Do not expose real customer, supplier, staff, account, or financial data.


1. PURCHASE LIST AND PURCHASE REPORTS
-------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Transactions > Purchase > All Purchase. Show the
purchase list, locate the demo purchase, and open its details. If available,
apply a date range or supplier filter and show the result. Show the print
action for the purchase or list if available.
Then open Reports > Transactions Report > Purchase Lines Report. Select a
short date range and, if available, a supplier or product filter. Run the
report and show the purchase line details.
If a purchase has been edited, open Reports > Audit Report > Purchase Edit
Trail and locate the demo record.

[NARRATION]
"All Purchase is the operational list for finding and reviewing purchase
invoices. We can locate a purchase and open its details, and print the
invoice or list where the available actions allow it.

"For a more detailed transaction view, the Purchase Lines Report shows
purchase line activity. We can choose a date range and use the filters
available in this report to narrow the results.

"When purchase editing is enabled, the Purchase Edit Trail provides an audit
view of purchase changes. Access to these reports and actions depends on the
user's permissions."


2. SALES LIST AND SALES REPORTS
-------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Transactions > Sales > All Sales. Show the list,
locate the demo sale, open it, and show its receipt-print option. Use a date
range, customer, payment status, or other filter if available; clear filters
after demonstrating.
Then open Reports > Transactions Report > Sales Lines Report. Apply a short
date range and a demo customer or product filter if available. Run the report
and show the line details.

[NARRATION]
"All Sales is where authorized users find and review sales invoices. We can
open a sale to inspect its details and print its receipt where available.

"The Sales Lines Report provides a detailed view of sold items. We can use
the filters offered by this report, such as a date range or customer, to
focus on the activity we want to review."


3. CUSTOMER AND SUPPLIER CREDIT REPORTS
---------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Reports > Transactions Report > Sales Credit Movement Report. Select a
date range and customer filter if available. Show the credit activity.
Then open A/R Aging under Reports > Accounting Reports and show the customer
receivables aging view.
Open Purchase Credit Movement Report and filter by date range or supplier if
available. Then open A/P Aging and show the supplier payables aging view.

[NARRATION]
"Credit movement reports help review customer credit created and paid over a
selected period. The Sales Credit Movement Report focuses on customer
receivables activity, while A/R Aging organizes outstanding customer
balances by age.

"For suppliers, the Purchase Credit Movement Report shows supplier credit
movement, and A/P Aging helps review outstanding payables by age. The
reports answer related but different questions: movement shows activity,
while aging focuses on current outstanding balances."


4. CASH MOVEMENT AND PAYMENT REPORTS
------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Reports > Transactions Report > Sales Cash Movement Report. Select a
date range and any available user/customer filters, then run the report.
Show Purchase Cash Movement Report next, using a matching date range. Point
out the difference between cash movement and invoice totals without
combining the two.

[NARRATION]
"Cash movement reports focus on recorded cash activity rather than simply
the total value of invoices. Sales Cash Movement and Purchase Cash Movement
let us review the related collections and payments for a selected period.
Available filters vary by report, so we will use only the options shown on
screen."


5. EXPENSE REPORT
-----------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Reports > Transactions Report > Expense Report. Select a date range and
an expense category if the filter is available. Run the report and show the
expense entries or summary. If supported, compare the report with the demo
expense voucher from the transaction list.

[NARRATION]
"The Expense Report helps review recorded expenses over time and by category.
We can use the date range and any available category filters to focus on
particular spending. The report is useful for checking expense activity
against the vouchers entered in the system."


6. CATEGORY, UNIT, PRODUCT, AND STOCK REPORTS
---------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Reports > Stock Report > Summary Stock Report. Show the available
product, category, and unit information, and apply a product, category, or
unit filter only if that control is present.
Open Product-wise Stock Trail and select the demo product to show its
movement history.
Open Stock Value Report and show the valuation columns that are available.
Explain that values use the report's displayed valuation basis; do not call
stock value realized revenue or profit.

[NARRATION]
"Stock reports connect product setup to inventory activity. The Summary
Stock Report provides a current stock summary by product, category, and
unit. The Product-wise Stock Trail shows movement history for an individual
product.

"The Stock Value Report provides an inventory valuation view. This is the
value of stock on hand using the system's stored product prices; it is not
the same as sales revenue or realized profit."

[RECORDING NOTE]
The report list documents product, category, and unit summaries. Do not
promise a separate report for each setup type or a particular filter unless
it is visibly available in the version being recorded.


7. STOCK ADJUSTMENT AND PRICE HISTORY REPORTS
---------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Reports > Stock Report > Stock Adjustment Report. Select a short date
range or a demo product if available and locate the stock adjustment shown
in the main walkthrough.
Then open Product Price History and locate a demo product whose price was
updated. Show the old and new price details or change history available on
screen.

[NARRATION]
"The Stock Adjustment Report records inventory changes entered outside
normal sales and purchases. We can locate the demo adjustment and review its
details using the filters provided.

"Product Price History helps review price changes after an update. Together,
these reports provide a useful record of inventory corrections and price
updates."


8. STOCK AND PURCHASE AUDIT REPORTS
-----------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Reports > Audit Report > Stock Movement Trail. Select the demo product
and a short date range if those filters are available. Show the activity
around the purchase, sale, or stock adjustment demonstrated earlier.
Open Purchase Edit Trail and show the change history for an edited demo
purchase, if one exists.

[NARRATION]
"Audit reports help investigate how records changed. Stock Movement Trail
shows product movement history, while Purchase Edit Trail shows purchase
change history. These views are useful when reviewing inventory activity
or tracing an authorized purchase edit."


9. CASH RECONCILIATION REPORT AND END-OF-DAY REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Transactions > Cash Reconcile. Show the Cash
Reconciliations list. Set a From Date and To Date to narrow the business
dates, then use Accounting Status to filter for Posted, Unposted, or Reversed
entries. If signed in as a cashier, show that the list is restricted to the
cashier's own reconciliations.
Open a demo reconciliation. Show its business date, cashier, status,
opening float, expected cash sales, expected cash expenses, expected closing
cash, physical counted cash, variance, and variance type. Show approval
details if the reconciliation is approved. Return to the list and, if
relevant, open related daily transaction or cash movement reports for the
same date.

[NARRATION]
"The Cash Reconciliations list provides a report-style view of daily cash
reconciliations. We can filter by business date and accounting posting
status, then open an entry to review its expected cash sales and expenses,
expected closing cash, physical count, variance, and variance type.

"The detail view also shows whether a reconciliation is submitted, approved
and locked, or reopened, along with approval details when available. Cashiers
see their own reconciliations, while authorized managers and supervisors can
review reconciliations across cashiers. Related transaction and cash movement
reports can provide context for the same business date."

[RECORDING NOTE]
This reconciliation report is the Cash Reconciliations list under
Transactions, not a separate item under the Reports menu. Its date and
accounting-status filters and record details are permission-controlled.


10. ACCOUNTING REPORTS FOR BANK, CAPITAL, AND LOAN ACTIVITY
----------------------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Reports > Accounting Reports. Show Trial Balance, then General Ledger.
If possible, select the relevant demo account and a date range that includes
the bank, owner capital, or loan entry from the earlier walkthrough.
Show Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow for a suitable period.
Show A/R Aging and A/P Aging only if they have not already been demonstrated.
Use the available print or PDF export action for the Balance Sheet if present.

[NARRATION]
"Accounting reports bring account-level activity and business position
together. Trial Balance summarizes debit and credit balances, and General
Ledger shows activity for individual accounts. These reports can help trace
the accounting entries associated with bank, capital, and loan activity.

"The Income Statement summarizes income and expenses. The Balance Sheet
shows assets, liabilities, and equity, and Cash Flow summarizes cash
movement. A Balance Sheet print or PDF export is available where supported.
The specific account filters and output options depend on the report."


11. REPORTS QUICK TOUR
----------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Show the Reports menu and briefly visit Transactions Report, Stock Report,
Audit Report, and Accounting Reports. Pause on the report names relevant to
the earlier demonstrations. End on the report most useful to the intended
audience.

[NARRATION]
"DarpPOS Credit groups its reports into transaction, stock, audit, and
accounting sections. Transaction reports cover sales, purchases, cash and
credit movement, profit, ledger summaries, and expenses. Stock reports
cover inventory balances, valuation, adjustments, movement, and price
history. Audit reports support review of purchase edits and stock movement.
Accounting reports provide financial statements, ledger details, and
receivables and payables aging.

"Report access is permission-based, and each report may offer different
filters and print or export options. This walkthrough demonstrates the
reports available to the selected demo role."
