DARPPOS CREDIT - VIDEO PROMOTION RECORDING SCRIPT
=================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Use this script as a guide while recording a product walkthrough of DarpPOS
Credit. The bracketed directions are on-screen actions; the quoted passages
are suggested narration. Adjust wording to match your speaking style and the
version of the application being recorded.

BEFORE RECORDING
----------------
- Use a demo environment and fictional customers, suppliers, products, and
  transaction data. Do not show real passwords, personal information, or
  confidential business figures.
- Sign in with an account that has the permissions needed for every section.
- Prepare a few demo products, a customer, a supplier, an expense category,
  and the relevant accounting accounts. Prepare a second authorized user if
  you want to demonstrate reconciliation approval.
- Confirm that the menus and actions are available in the environment. Some
  options depend on role permissions and system settings.
- Keep the browser zoom and window size consistent. Pause briefly after each
  save or report load so the viewer can read the result.
- Use the on-screen totals and status labels rather than narrating made-up
  amounts. Printing and report formats may depend on the page and permissions.


1. OPENING AND GENERAL OVERVIEW
------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Show the DarpPOS Credit sign-in page or application logo. Sign in using the
demo account. Give a brief, steady view of the Dashboard, then the sidebar.
Point out the major sections without lingering on private data.

[NARRATION]
"Welcome to DarpPOS Credit, a point-of-sale and business management system
for managing sales, purchases, inventory, customer and supplier credit,
expenses, cash reconciliation, accounting, and reports.

"The system brings day-to-day transactions and management tools together.
What each user can see and do depends on their assigned role and permissions.
In this walkthrough, we will follow common business workflows, from recording
transactions to reviewing reports and managing accounts."


2. PURCHASE ENTRY, PURCHASE REPORT, EDITING, AND PRINTING
--------------------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Transactions > Purchase > Create. Add a demo
product, enter its quantity and purchase cost, and review the line and invoice
totals. Select a supplier and a payment status. Submit the purchase.
Then open Purchase > All Purchase, locate the new record, open it, and show
the available edit and print actions. Edit only a safe demo record if editing
is enabled. Show the print preview or print action without printing private
information.

[NARRATION]
"A purchase records goods received from a supplier and increases product
stock. From Purchase Create, we select the items, enter quantities and costs,
review the total, and choose the supplier and payment status.

"After saving, All Purchase provides a list of recorded purchases. Depending
on permissions and the purchase-edit settings, an eligible purchase can be
reviewed or edited. We can also open and print a purchase record or print the
list for reference."

[RECORDING NOTE]
Purchase editing may be disabled, restricted by role, or limited by the
configured edit window. If so, show the restriction accurately instead of
implying every purchase can always be edited.


3. PAYING SUPPLIER CREDIT AND PARTIAL PURCHASE INVOICES
------------------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Payments > Credit Purchases List. Find the demo
supplier or outstanding purchase. Open its balance, enter a payment smaller
than the outstanding balance to demonstrate a partial payment, and save.
Show the remaining balance. If convenient, demonstrate a second payment that
clears the remaining balance.

[NARRATION]
"When a purchase is only partly paid, the unpaid amount remains as supplier
credit. From Credit Purchases List, we select the relevant supplier or
purchase, enter the payment being made, and save it. The outstanding balance
is reduced by that payment. A later payment can clear the remaining balance."


4. SALES ENTRY, SALES REPORT, AND PRINTING
------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Transactions > Sales > Create. Add a demo product
by selecting it or scanning its barcode. Enter quantity, review the price and
total, and select a demo customer. Choose Full Payment for this example and
submit the sale. Open Sales > All Sales, locate the sale, and show the list,
record details, receipt print action, and sales-list print action if
available.

[NARRATION]
"Sales entry is designed to record items sold and the payment status in one
workflow. We add the products, confirm quantities and prices, select the
customer, and choose how the invoice is being paid.

"All Sales lets authorized users review sales records. From here, we can open
a sale and print its receipt, or print the sales list where that option is
available."


5. PAYING CUSTOMER CREDIT AND PARTIAL SALES INVOICES
---------------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Payments > Credit Sales List. Select the demo
customer or outstanding sale. Enter a payment smaller than the amount due,
save, and show the reduced balance.

[NARRATION]
"If a customer makes a partial payment, the remaining amount stays
outstanding on the credit account. In Credit Sales List, we select the
customer or invoice, record the amount received, and save. The balance
updates to reflect what remains due."


6. SALES RECONCILIATION, APPROVALS, AND REPORTS
-----------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Transactions > Cash Reconcile > Create. Show the
business date, opening float, expected cash values, and the counted cash
field. Enter demo values and notes, then submit. Show the calculated closing
amount and variance. Sign in as, or switch to, an authorized approver if the
demo setup permits. Open the submitted reconciliation, approve it, and show
the approved/locked status. Open the relevant reconciliation report or
available report page and show its filters and results.

[NARRATION]
"Cash reconciliation compares the system's expected cash with the amount
physically counted. We confirm the business date and opening float, review
the expected cash activity, enter the counted amount, and submit the
reconciliation. The system calculates the expected closing cash and shows
whether there is a variance.

"An authorized manager or supervisor can review and approve a submitted
reconciliation. Approved reconciliations are locked; an authorized user must
reverse approval before making an edit. Reconciliation and transaction
reports help management review activity and exceptions."

[RECORDING NOTE]
Use a separate authorized account for approval. Do not suggest that every
user can approve reconciliations. Show the report actually available in the
recording environment; report menus depend on permissions.


7. CREATING EXPENSES
--------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Transactions > Expenses. Create an expense using
a demo expense category, date, description, and payment information shown by
the form. Save it. Open the expense list or Expenses Report and show the new
entry. Show the expense voucher print option if available.

[NARRATION]
"Expenses can be recorded and organized by category, making routine costs
easier to review. We enter the details for this demo expense and save it.
Expense records can then be reviewed in the expense list and transaction
reports, with printing available where supported."


8. CATEGORIES, UNITS, PRODUCTS, AND RELATED REPORTS
--------------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Setups > Manage Setups > Category. Create a demo category, then open its
list and edit the demo record. Save the change.
Repeat under Unit: create a demo unit, edit it, and save.
Open Product: create a demo product and select the category and unit just
created. Enter the low-stock threshold and submit. Open the product list,
edit the demo product, and save.
Then open Reports > Stock Report. Show the Summary Stock Report and filter
or locate the demo product/category/unit if the report provides those
controls. Also show Product-wise Stock Trail for the product, if available.

[NARRATION]
"Categories group products into useful business sections, and units describe
how products are measured, such as pieces, boxes, or kilograms. We can create
and update these setup records as the business changes.

"A product is linked to its category and unit, and can have a low-stock
threshold to support inventory monitoring. After creating and updating a
product, we can review stock information in the Summary Stock Report and
inspect its movement in the Product-wise Stock Trail. Report filters and
available detail vary by report."

[RECORDING NOTE]
The manual documents stock reports for product, category, and unit summaries
and product movement history. Do not present a category-specific or
unit-specific report screen unless it is present in the version being
recorded.


9. UPDATING PRODUCT PRICES
-------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Setups > Manage Setups > Update Price. Locate a demo product, show its
current price fields, update the relevant price, and save. Then open Reports >
Stock Report > Product Price History and show the recorded change if it
appears.

[NARRATION]
"Product prices can be reviewed and updated from Update Price. After saving
a change, Product Price History provides a way to review price changes over
time."


10. STOCK ADJUSTMENT AND REPORT
-------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Transactions > Manage Transactions > Stock > Create. Select a demo
product, enter an appropriate adjustment quantity and a clear reason, review
the effect, and submit. Open All Stock Adjustments and show the new record.
Then open Reports > Stock Report > Stock Adjustment Report and locate the
adjustment.

[NARRATION]
"Stock adjustments are for inventory changes outside normal purchases and
sales, such as damaged goods or a stock-count correction. We select the
product, enter the adjustment and reason, and review the effect before
submitting. The adjustment list and Stock Adjustment Report provide a record
for review."


11. CREATING AND UPDATING USERS AND ROLES
-----------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open User Setup > Manage Users. Create a demo user, assign an appropriate
role, and save. Open the user list and update the demo user's details or
assigned role. Then open Roles, show a role's permissions, and create or edit
a demo role if the environment allows. Save and show the updated role.

[NARRATION]
"User Setup lets administrators manage login accounts and roles. We can
create a user and assign the role that matches their responsibilities.
Roles determine which menus and actions are available, including transaction
entry, editing, approvals, reports, and administration. Review permissions
carefully before saving changes."

[RECORDING NOTE]
Use fictional login details and never reveal a working password. Do not
change or display permissions on real users during the recording.


12. CREATING AND UPDATING CUSTOMER AND SUPPLIER ACCOUNTS
-------------------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Under Setups > Manage Setups, open Customers. Create a demo customer, save
it, locate it in the customer list, and edit the demo record.
Repeat under Suppliers: create a demo supplier, save it, locate it, and edit
the demo record.
If useful, show where a customer or supplier can be selected during a sale
or purchase.

[NARRATION]
"Customer accounts support sales and customer credit tracking. Supplier
accounts support purchase entry and supplier credit tracking. We can create
and update these records in Setups, then select them on the relevant sales
or purchase transactions."


13. CREATING BANK ACCOUNTS AND CAPITAL OR LOAN ENTRIES
-----------------------------------------------------

[ON SCREEN]
Open Accounting Setup > Chart of Accounts and show the relevant active
bank-type account, if one is already configured. Open Accounting Setup >
Bank Accounts, create a demo bank account with its name, currency, opening
balance, and linked GL account as appropriate. Save and show the bank account
list.
Then open Accounting Setup > Capital & Loans. Create a demo Owner Capital
entry or a demo loan entry, choose whether funds are received into cash or a
bank account, complete the required source/lender fields for a loan, and
save. Show the resulting entry or account balance where available.

[NARRATION]
"Bank Accounts are configured in Accounting Setup and linked to a bank-type
general ledger account. The setup records include the account name, currency,
opening balance, and account status.

"Capital & Loans records funds introduced into the business as owner capital
or as a bank or individual loan. We choose where the funds are received,
complete the entry details, and save. Loan entries require lender or source
information. When funds are received into a bank account, the system updates
that bank balance and posts the related accounting entry."

[RECORDING NOTE]
Use fictitious account details and amounts. Bank accounts with related
transactions or capital/loan entries may not be deletable.


14. CLOSING
-----------

[ON SCREEN]
Return to the Dashboard or a clean system overview. End on the application
name or logo.

[NARRATION]
"From daily sales and purchasing to inventory, credit management, expenses,
cash reconciliation, accounting, and reporting, DarpPOS Credit helps bring
core business operations into one system. Access is controlled by roles, so
teams can work with the tools relevant to their responsibilities.

"Thank you for watching this overview of DarpPOS Credit."
